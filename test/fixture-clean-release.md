# Fixture — a clean outbound document (negative control)

This file exists to prove releasecheck.py does NOT block the sentences the practice is
built on. Every line below is wording the firm actually uses on a client surface, in
the engagement letter, or in the referral protocol. If a line here starts blocking, the
gate has begun crying wolf, which teaches its reader to skip BLOCK.

## The scope reservation, as stated to the Division and on the site
We would not recommend the purchase or sale of any security. We do not recommend, and
this desk contains no recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security or other
instrument. Management determines any resulting portfolio, funding or hedging action.
Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any instrument.

## The referral protocol, exercised on a call
The CFO asked where to reinvest the maturities rolling off this quarter. The firm does
not make that recommendation; the question belongs with the institution's investment
manager, broker-dealer or registered adviser. The firm offered to measure the
consequence of any option the institution names, against its own limits.

## A withheld finding, in the four-part convention
Finding: $125M of CDs reprices this quarter at a weighted 4.35%. Management question:
what renewal rate does the board's pricing authority permit? Client decision: management
determines any resulting portfolio, funding or hedging action.
Withheld: anything that says the institution should buy, sell, hold, replace, hedge, extend or shorten a security or a maturity exposure.
(The corrective context is read per LINE, as the source comments say: a withheld line must carry its own marker.)

## Ordinary treasury language that is measurement, not a verdict
The 5Y point sits at 3.71% today. Duration of the AFS book is 3.2 years. The extended
term of the engagement runs eight weeks. Replaced by: reduce cash. The renewal defense
band is 25 to 50 bp. Extend the engagement by change order.
