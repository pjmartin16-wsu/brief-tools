Treasury Full Desk Read

September CPI lands Wednesday with the 2-year 92 bp above the 3.88% effective funds rate and the long end above 5%.
Tuesday, October 13, 2026 · Market data as of Oct 9 close · Fed funds 3.75–4.00% (raised Sep 16)
The Read

Wednesday's September CPI is the week's test, and the inflation backdrop going into it is still above the 2% goal on every measure: August headline CPI 3.4% and August core CPI 2.4%, with August headline PCE 3.4% and August core PCE 3.0%. The labor side is softer: September payrolls rose 29,000 and the September unemployment rate was 4.2%. The FOMC raised the target range a quarter point to 3.75–4.00% on September 16 by a 12–0 vote, and the September projections put the 2026 median for the federal funds rate at 4.1% (central tendency 4.1–4.4%).

Friday's close moved the front of the curve more than the long end. The 2-year closed at 4.80%, 5 bp higher than the day before, and the 10-year at 5.24%, 2 bp higher than the day before; the 30-year was unchanged at 5.60%. On the week the 2-year is 3 bp lower than Oct 2 and the 10-year 4 bp lower, leaving 2s10s at +44 bp. The 2-year sits 92 bp above the 3.88% effective funds rate.

For the sample bank the same levels cut both ways. New-money yields above 5% across the belly and long end sit well above its 2.95% securities book yield. The same yields hold its $410M of fixed-rate AFS securities under pressure, which is where AOCI (−$41.4M) and the EVE profile register it: the +300 EVE result of −14.6% sits beyond the internal −13.75% watch trigger but within the −25% policy limit. Funding has not eased either. The top of Bankrate's one-year CD list is 4.75% against the sample bank's 4.09% weighted CD cost.

Today's Tie-In
 About $125M of securities cash flow comes due over the next twelve months against a 2.95% book yield. Each 25 bp between that book yield and whatever it earns next is on the order of $313k a year, gross, full-year, full deployment ($125M × 0.25%).
The $155M Q3 tranche at ~4.35% reprices against the sample bank's own 4.00–4.10% renewal defense band; renewing 25–35 bp below the tranche rate is ~$0.4–0.5M a year on that tranche.
AOCI sits at −$41.4M, up $4.6M over three months. Roughly $4.3M of AOCI moves per 25 bp, taken at the whole securities portfolio's 4.2-year effective duration (the book states no AFS-only duration); yields above 5% across the belly and long end keep that mark under pressure.
The +300 EVE position is −14.6%, beyond the internal −13.75% watch trigger but within the −25% policy limit, on a balance sheet carrying $560M of securities and $265M of equity; it is the figure to carry into the next ALCO review.
