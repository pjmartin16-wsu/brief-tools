THE READ
Wednesday's September CPI is the week's test, and the inflation backdrop going into it is still above the 2% goal on every measure: August headline CPI 3.4% and August core CPI 2.4%, with August headline PCE 3.4% and August core PCE 3.0%. The labor side is softer: September payrolls rose 29,000 and the September unemployment rate was 4.2%. The FOMC raised the target range a quarter point to 3.75-4.00% on September 16 by a 12-0 vote, and the September projections put the 2026 median for the federal funds rate at 4.1% (central tendency 4.1-4.4%).

Friday's close moved the front of the curve more than the long end. The 2-year closed at 4.80%, 5 bp higher than the day before, and the 10-year at 5.24%, 2 bp higher than the day before; the 30-year was unchanged at 5.60%. On the week the 2-year is 3 bp lower than Oct 2 and the 10-year 4 bp lower, leaving 2s10s at +44 bp. The 2-year sits 92 bp above the 3.88% effective funds rate.

For a deposit-funded balance sheet the same levels cut both ways. With the 5-year at 5.02% and the 10-year at 5.24%, new-money yields across the belly and long end are above 5%, and those same yields hold the market value of fixed-rate AFS securities down: both register in the next EVE and AOCI run. Funding has not eased either. The top of Bankrate's one-year CD list is 4.75% and its top high-yield savings rate 4.27%, advertised consumer yields that frame where renewal betas get tested.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOUR BALANCE SHEET
- Deposits: Renewal pricing is being set against a 4.75% top one-year CD and a 4.27% top high-yield savings rate on Bankrate's lists. A treasury team would measure the gap between maturing CD rates and offered renewal rates, and what each point of deposit beta does to the cost of deposits and to NII.
- Investments: New-money yields above 5% across the belly and long end lift reinvestment income, and the same yields keep fixed-rate AFS marks under pressure. Measure the pickup on maturing cash flow against the existing book yield, and the AOCI change per 25 bp of rate move.
- IRR / ALCO: With 2s10s at +44 bp and the 2-year 92 bp above the 3.88% effective funds rate, re-run NII and EVE at current levels and check NEV and 5300 or call-report IRR measures against policy limits; the rising-rate shocks are where fixed-rate duration shows up.
