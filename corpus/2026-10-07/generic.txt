THE READ
The September jobs report came in soft - nonfarm payrolls rose +29,000 and the unemployment rate ticked to 4.2% - while inflation held above target: August headline CPI 3.4% and core CPI 2.4%, with August headline PCE 3.4% and core PCE 3.0%. Against that mix the FOMC raised the target range a quarter point to 3.75-4.00% at its September 16 meeting, a unanimous decision, and the September projections put the published year-end 2026 federal-funds median at 4.1% (central tendency 4.1-4.4%).

The long end is doing work of its own. Tuesday's par curve has the 10-year at 5.27% and the 30-year at 5.64%, with the 5-year at 5.03% - the belly and long end above 5% while the effective funds rate is 3.88%. Yields eased 2-5 basis points across the curve on the day, leaving 2s10s at +48 bp, one basis point steeper than Monday. The 2-year at 4.79% stands about 91 bp above the 3.88% effective funds rate.

For a deposit-funded balance sheet the two forces pull in opposite directions and both belong in the next ALCO run. New-money and reinvestment yields across the belly and long end are above 5%, while the market value of fixed-rate AFS securities sits lower as long yields hold above 5% - higher new-money yields and lower AFS marks show up in the same EVE and AOCI run. Deposit competition has not relaxed: the top of Bankrate's one-year CD list is 4.75% and its top high-yield savings rate 4.27%, the frame against which renewal betas are tested.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOUR BALANCE SHEET
Deposits: Renewal betas are being tested against a 4.75% top one-year CD APY and a 4.27% top high-yield savings APY on Bankrate's lists - advertised consumer yields, not a cost of funds, but the competitive frame renewal pricing is modeled against. Measure the gap between maturing CD rates and today's offered renewal rates, and what each basis point of beta does to the cost of deposits.
Investments: New-money and reinvestment yields across the belly and long end are above 5% while fixed-rate AFS market values sit lower as long yields hold above 5%: both show up in the next EVE and AOCI run. Measure reinvestment pickup against the existing book yield and the AOCI sensitivity per 25 bp of rate move.
IRR / ALCO: With the curve positively sloped (2s10s +48 bp) and the front end near a 3.88% effective funds rate, re-run NII and EVE at current levels and check NEV and 5300/call-report IRR measures against policy limits - the +300 scenario is where fixed-rate duration shows up most.
