The Read

The Federal Reserve raised the federal funds target range by a quarter point to 3.75–4.00% on Sep 16, a 12–0 vote, and the September projections put the median federal funds rate at 4.1% for the end of 2026 (central tendency 4.1–4.4%). August headline CPI ran 3.4% and core CPI 2.4%; August headline PCE ran 3.4% and core PCE 3.0%; September nonfarm payrolls rose 29,000 and the September unemployment rate was 4.2%. September CPI is due Wednesday, Oct 14, the week's main data point.

Treasury's Oct 9 close put the 2-year at 4.80%, the 10-year at 5.24% and the 30-year at 5.60%, leaving 2s10s at +44 bp, 3 bp flatter than the day before. On the week, the 10-year is down 4 bp and the 2-year down 3 bp, while the 3-month bill is up 6 bp at 4.25%. Overnight money sits inside the range: EFFR printed 3.88% on Oct 8, with IORB at 3.90%.

For the illustrative ~$2.75B bank, the week's small decline in intermediate yields moves AOCI only at the margin: sensitivity is about $4.3M per 25 bp on the $410M AFS book, taken at the whole securities portfolio's 4.2-year effective duration (the book states no AFS-only duration). The larger exposure is on the funding side, where the $155M Q3 tranche at ~4.35% reprices against the sample bank's own 4.00–4.10% renewal defense band while the top 12-month CD APY on Bankrate's list is 4.75%. Under the +300 shock, EVE at −14.6% is beyond the internal −13.75% watch trigger but within the −25% policy limit.
